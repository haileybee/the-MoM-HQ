Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This is a launch-ready starter policy for The Mom Hub and should be reviewed before public launch, especially once the final hosting, analytics, authentication, AI, and payment providers are connected.

Information we may collect

The Mom Hub may collect account information, household settings, cleaning tasks, pantry and grocery information, child-profile information supplied by a parent or guardian, laundry information, photos users choose to upload, subscription status, device information, and support communications.

How information is used

Information is used to provide and sync app features, create user-requested household plans, save preferences, improve reliability, provide support, prevent abuse, and manage subscriptions.

Photos

Users may choose to upload household, pantry, reward, profile, or laundry photos. The app should explain when photos are stored or sent to an AI service. Users should be able to delete stored photos. Future AI analysis providers will be disclosed here before that feature is launched publicly.

Children's information

Child profiles are created and managed by the household account holder. The Mom Hub is designed as a tool for parents and guardians, not as a service for children to independently create accounts.

Payments

Payment details should be collected and processed by the connected payment processor. The Mom Hub should not directly store full card numbers or card security codes.

Data sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate features such as authentication, hosting, cloud storage, AI analysis, payment processing, security, and customer support. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Data choices

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of account information where applicable. In-app deletion tools may also be provided for household records and photos.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used or planned, including authenticated accounts, private data access controls, secure connections, and payment processing through a specialized provider.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the customer-support method listed on our Support page.
