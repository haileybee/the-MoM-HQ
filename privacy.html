Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

MoMHQ is designed to keep private household information private and to minimize how long sensitive scan data is kept. Information that a user intentionally saves remains available to that household until it is deleted or the account is removed.

Information we collect

MoMHQ may collect account information, household settings, cleaning tasks, pantry and grocery information, child-profile information supplied by a parent or guardian, ChoreCoin activity, laundry information, schedules, pet and medication records, subscription status, device information, support communications, and photos or images a user intentionally selects for an app feature.

How information is used

Information is used to provide and sync requested app features, create household plans and AI-assisted results, save user preferences and household records, improve reliability and security, provide support, prevent abuse, and manage subscriptions.

Ephemeral AI image processing

When a user submits an image for AI-assisted processing, such as a Smart Cleaning room scan or receipt/pantry scan, MoMHQ is designed to treat the processing copy as temporary. MoMHQ's current AI processing functions receive the image for the requested scan, send it to the configured AI provider, return the generated result, and do not save a separate server-side copy of that submitted image to MoMHQ's database or Supabase Storage. Responses from these processing functions are marked not to be cached.

Some app features may separately allow a user to intentionally save a photo as part of a household record, profile, reward, receipt record, or other feature. A photo intentionally saved by the user is persistent household data rather than an ephemeral processing copy and remains until the user deletes it or the associated data is removed.

AI service providers

MoMHQ may use third-party AI providers to perform user-requested image analysis. Those providers process submitted data according to their own service terms, privacy policies, and configured data-retention settings. MoMHQ aims to use production configurations that minimize retention and does not intentionally use submitted household images for advertising.

Children's information

Child profiles are created and managed by an adult household account holder. MoMHQ is designed as a household tool for parents and guardians, not as a service for children to independently create public accounts. Child-profile information is intended to remain within the authorized household unless an adult intentionally shares information through a separate feature.

Data storage and household syncing

Information a user intentionally saves, including chores, ChoreCoins, pantry items, recipes, schedules, household settings, pet records, medication records, and other household records, may be stored so the app can sync across authorized devices and household members. MoMHQ separates private household information from public community features.

Payments

Payment details are processed by the applicable app store or connected payment provider. MoMHQ does not need to store full card numbers or card security codes to provide subscription access.

Data sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate requested features such as authentication, hosting, syncing, AI analysis, payment processing, security, and customer support. Information may also be disclosed when required by law. MoMHQ does not sell user conversations or household data to advertisers.

Data choices and deletion

Users may edit or delete supported household records in the app and may request account deletion where applicable. Account deletion is intended to remove the user's MoMHQ account and associated stored household data, subject to limited information that may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record requirements.

Security

MoMHQ uses or is designed to use authenticated accounts, household-scoped access controls, encrypted network connections, server-side API secrets, and other reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Sensitive provider keys are kept on the server side rather than embedded in the Android app.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the customer-support method listed on our Support page.
