Delete Your MoMHQ Account

Last updated: August 26, 2026

MoMHQ users can delete their account and associated account data from inside the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open MoMHQ and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open Profile & Account.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the warning and confirm the deletion request.

Deleting your account is intended to remove the account and personal data associated with it, including profile information and household data tied to that account, except for information that may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, billing, or other legitimate operational reasons.

Child and household data

If your account created or managed child profiles or household records, associated records controlled by your account may also be deleted or removed when the account is deleted. Information that belongs to other household members may remain in their accounts where applicable.

If you cannot access the app

If you are unable to access your account, use the MoMHQ Support page for help with an account-deletion request. Do not send passwords or payment-card information in a support request.

What happens after deletion

Once an account-deletion request is completed, the account should no longer be usable. Some information may remain temporarily in backups before being removed through normal backup-rotation procedures.

Privacy

For more information about how MoMHQ handles personal information, see the Privacy Policy.
