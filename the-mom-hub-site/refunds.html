Refund & Dispute Policy

The Mom Hub Premium is billed monthly after any applicable free trial.

No refunds for completed billing periods

Subscription payments are non-refundable once charged. We do not provide full or partial refunds or prorated credits for unused time in a billing period.

You may cancel at any time. After cancellation, you keep Premium access through the end of the billing period already paid for and will not be charged for the following month.

Billing problems

If you believe a charge was made in error, contact customer support so the issue can be reviewed.
