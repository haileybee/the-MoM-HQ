Cancellation Policy

You may cancel The Mom Hub Premium at any time.

What happens when you cancel

  • Your subscription will not renew for the following billing period.
  • You will not be charged again after cancellation unless you choose to resubscribe.
  • You will continue to have access to Premium features until the end of the billing period you already paid for.
  • No refund or prorated credit is provided for the current billing period or unused time.

Example: if your monthly Premium subscription renews on August 5 and you cancel on August 12, you keep Premium access until September 5 and will not be charged again on September 5.
